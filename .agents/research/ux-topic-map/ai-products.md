# UX of AI features and agents - landscape scout

## Coverage and gaps

Fetched and read: Microsoft's eighteen guidelines, HAX Toolkit landing page, Google PAIR (explainability and errors chapters), IBM Carbon for AI, NN/g prompt-controls article and response-time limits, Shape of AI home, the MCP tools specification (2025-06-18 and 2025-11-25), MCP Apps overview, the OpenAI Apps SDK UI guidelines and UX principles, the Vercel AI SDK tool-usage page, AI Act article 50 text, the Commission code-of-practice page, OWASP LLM01:2025, Anthropic autonomy and sandboxing posts, three arXiv abstracts, Willison's lethal trifecta.

Not obtained, so no claim is made from them: Apple HIG generative-AI and machine-learning pages (page returned only a title; a second fetch was rate-limited), assistant-ui, Anthropic's own assistant design guidance, OpenAI's tool-approval guidance beyond the Apps SDK pages, the text of the Commission's article 50 guidelines (the page lists them as published but gives no content or date), any latency or streaming-perception study. WebSearch was exhausted for the session, so no discovery by search. Those gaps are candidates for a deep-dive, not findings.

## Candidate topics

Priority is for a project of open-source developer tools (CLIs, Astro and MkDocs sites, a Vite GUI) that increasingly ship MCP servers, skills and chat or agent features. "Covered" means by css-theming, docs-quality, code-docs or the CLI contracts; none of the candidates below is covered by them except where noted.

| 1 | Where must a tool-call approval be required, and what must the approval dialog show (tool name, full arguments, destructive or not)? | The MCP spec says there SHOULD always be a human able to deny invocations, and clients SHOULD show tool inputs before calling to prevent exfiltration. | 3 | How is approval fatigue avoided without removing oversight (sandbox, scoped allow-lists, automatic decisions that stay visible)? | Anthropic reports sandboxing cut permission prompts 84 percent and calls out inattentive approval; its autonomy study shows auto-approve rising from about 20 percent to over 40 percent of sessions with use. | https://www.anthropic.com/engineering/claude-code-sandboxing ; https://www.anthropic.com/research/measuring-agent-autonomy | no | high | Partly. | 4 | Must automatic approval decisions be shown as automatic? | AI SDK exposes `approval.isAutomatic` and `reason`; hiding them removes the audit trail the user relies on. | https://ai-sdk.dev/docs/ai-sdk-ui/chatbot-tool-usage | no | medium | Yes. | 5 | How are untrusted tool results and retrieved content separated and labelled from instructions in the interface and context? | OWASP LLM01:2025 lists "segregate and clearly label untrusted external content" and "require human approval for high-risk actions" as mitigations; no method is foolproof. | https://genai.owasp.org/llmrisk/llm01-prompt-injection/ | no | high | Partly. | 8 | Is the user told they are talking to an AI, and when (article 50(1): at the latest at first interaction, unless obvious)? | Legal duty on providers from 2 August 2026 for systems meant to interact directly with people; must be clear, distinguishable, and accessible. | https://artificialintelligenceact.eu/article/50/ | no | high | Yes. | 9 | Is generated content marked machine-readably (article 50(2)) and labelled where it is a deepfake or public-interest text (50(4))? | Providers must mark synthetic audio, image, video or text as machine-readable; deployers must disclose deepfakes and public-interest text unless under editorial review. | 10 | What is the current state of the article 50 obligations (code of practice, guidelines, omnibus, any grace period)? | The Commission page lists a final code on 10 June 2026 and application 2 August 2026; an AI omnibus entered into force 27 July 2026 but the page did not state its effect on article 50. | 11 | How much of what the model can do and how well should be stated up front (guidelines 1 and 2)? | First two of Microsoft's eighteen guidelines; NN/g found users had to ask the bot what it could do, and one gave up on a summarization task. | https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/articles/guidelines-for-human-ai-interaction-eighteen-best-practices-for-human-centered-ai-design/ ; https://www.nngroup.com/articles/prompt-controls-genai/ | no | medium | Partly. Check an empty state offers capability hints or example prompts; a stated limit is present. Quality of the hints is judgment. |
| 12 | When should confidence be displayed, and in which form (categorical, n-best, numeric)? | PAIR: skip confidence if it does not change decisions or may mislead; numeric values like 97 percent can look wrong; categorical needs defined cutoffs tied to actions. | https://pair.withgoogle.com/chapter/explainability-trust/ | no | medium | Partly. Check that any confidence display documents the action each level implies; check no unexplained bare percentage. |
| 13 | Does an explanation next to an AI output help or increase overreliance? | Bansal et al. found explanations raised acceptance of AI advice whether or not it was correct and did not improve team accuracy. A rule can require that explanation is on demand (Carbon) and test-measured, not default. |
| 14 | Which uncertainty wording does an assistant use in text, and was it tested? | A pre-registered study (N=404) found first-person hedges ("I'm not sure, but") reduced trust and accepted wrong answers; a generic hedge was weaker and not significant; the authors say to user-test phrasing. | https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.00623 | no | medium | Partly. Check system-prompt or UI strings for a hedge convention; effect needs a test with users. |
| 15 | Which citation display is acceptable given how often citations fail to support claims? | Liu et al. measured 51.5 percent of sentences fully supported and 74.5 percent of citations supporting their sentence in four audited engines (2023 systems; dated). A citation chip is not proof. | https://arxiv.org/abs/2304.09848 | no | high | Partly. Check each citation links to the exact passage or source, opens verifiably, and that the UI does not present citations as verification; a sampling eval of support rate is runnable. |
| 16 | Should the AI label be a persistent, accessible control that opens an explanation, and must AI styling be reserved? | Carbon: mark all AI usage, label is an interactive trigger for an explainability popover, styling must not be decorative. | https://carbondesignsystem.com/guidelines/carbon-for-ai/ | no | medium | Yes. Check every AI-generated element carries the label component and the label is keyboard-focusable. |
| 17 | After a user edits AI-suggested content, can they restore the original ("revert to AI")? | Carbon makes this a component rule; it is also an undo path for generated content. | https://carbondesignsystem.com/guidelines/carbon-for-ai/ | no | medium | Yes. Check an edit-after-generate flow retains the original and exposes a restore action. |
| 18 | Which agent actions need undo, preview or a plan before execution, and which are irreversible? | Shape of AI lists Action plan, Verification, Branches, Cost estimates and Draft mode as governors; Anthropic reports 0.8 percent of observed API tool calls irreversible. | https://www.shapeof.ai/ ; https://www.anthropic.com/research/measuring-agent-autonomy | no | high | Partly. | 19 | Are tool annotations (read-only, destructive hints) used to drive approval, given the spec says they are untrusted? | The MCP spec says clients MUST treat annotations as untrusted unless the server is trusted; a UI that skips confirmation on a `readOnly` hint from a third-party server is exploitable. | 20 | Is a log of tool usage kept and shown (audit trail), and can the user trace from prompt to result? | MCP: clients SHOULD log tool usage for audit; Shape of AI "Footprints" and "Stream of Thought". | https://modelcontextprotocol.io/specification/2025-11-25/server/tools ; https://www.shapeof.ai/ | no | medium | Yes. | 21 | What are the safe-error rules for tool and stream errors (what the user sees, what the model sees)? | AI SDK sends the same error text to client and model by default and can leak host names or paths; `redactToolErrors` changes stored history and can cause rejected requests with some Anthropic models. | 22 | What do tool execution errors versus protocol errors return so a model can self-correct? | MCP: execution errors with `isError: true` carry actionable text; clients SHOULD feed them to the model. | https://modelcontextprotocol.io/specification/2025-11-25/server/tools | no | medium | Yes. | 25 | Is an MCP App's CSP and permission request as narrow as possible? | MCP Apps run in a sandboxed iframe with `_meta.ui.csp` and `permissions`; OpenAI says keep the CSP allowlist narrow. | https://modelcontextprotocol.io/extensions/apps/overview ; https://developers.openai.com/apps-sdk/concepts/ui-guidelines | no | medium | Yes. | 29 | What happens when the AI fails: boring failure, return of control, alternative offered? | PAIR: failstates explain limits specifically, return control to the user, and keep failure boring to avoid helping bad actors; context errors feel like errors because behavior was unexplained. | https://pair.withgoogle.com/chapter/errors-failing/ | no | medium | Partly. | 30 | Is there a global control and a notice for model or behavior changes (guidelines 14, 17, 18)? | Microsoft: update cautiously, global controls, notify about changes. | 31 | Does the memory feature let users see, edit and turn off what the AI remembers (and use an incognito path)? | Shape of AI Memory, Data ownership and Incognito; Microsoft guideline 12 and 17; PAIR says explain what data is used and whether it can be removed. | https://www.shapeof.ai/ ; https://pair.withgoogle.com/chapter/explainability-trust/ | no | medium | Yes. | 32 | Is the AI chat or generated output accessible (live regions during streaming, keyboard, focus after generation, contrast)? | Article 50(5) requires disclosure to conform to accessibility requirements; OpenAI requires WCAG AA contrast, alt text, text resizing in widgets. ## Contested

| question | position A | position B |
|---|---|---|
| Should every tool call need approval? | MCP says a human SHOULD always be able to deny invocations and clients SHOULD confirm sensitive operations (https://modelcontextprotocol.io/specification/2025-11-25/server/tools); OWASP requires approval for high-risk actions (https://genai.owasp.org/llmrisk/llm01-prompt-injection/). | Anthropic argues "effective oversight doesn't require approving every action" and that constant prompts cause inattentive approval (https://www.anthropic.com/research/measuring-agent-autonomy ; https://www.anthropic.com/engineering/claude-code-sandboxing). Reconcile by tiering, not by removing the gate. |
| Do explanations improve decisions? | Carbon and PAIR treat explainability as a trust builder to integrate into the workflow (https://carbondesignsystem.com/guidelines/carbon-for-ai/ ; https://pair.withgoogle.com/chapter/explainability-trust/). | Bansal et al. found explanations increased acceptance regardless of correctness without better team accuracy (https://arxiv.org/abs/2006.14779). PAIR itself says tie depth to stakes. |
| Does hedged language help? | A pre-registered study found first-person hedges reduce overreliance (https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.00623). | The same study found a general hedge not significant and overreliance not eliminated; the authors say to test per product. |
| Should confidence be shown at all? | Microsoft guideline 2 says make clear how well the system can do what it does (https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/articles/guidelines-for-human-ai-interaction-eighteen-best-practices-for-human-centered-ai-design/). | PAIR says skip confidence when it does not change decisions or may mislead (https://pair.withgoogle.com/chapter/explainability-trust/). |
| Are keep-it-simple card limits consistent? | OpenAI sets carousel metadata at three lines max. | The same page also says avoid more than two lines (https://developers.openai.com/apps-sdk/concepts/ui-guidelines); the guidance is internally inconsistent. |
