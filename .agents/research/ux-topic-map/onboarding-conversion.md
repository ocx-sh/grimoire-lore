# UX onboarding and conversion flows — landscape scout

Assigned axis: the flows where design decides whether a user stays: sign-up,
first run, activation, pricing and checkout, cancellation. Rows on auth
protocols (5, 15 to 19, 20), experiment-validity statistics (28 to 33) and
developer-tool time to first success (2 to 4, 38) sit OUTSIDE or at the edge of
that axis. They are kept and tagged "adjacent" in the Axis map below so a
consolidator can route them to the auth, experimentation or devtool-docs
corpora; in-axis rows are 1, 6 to 14, 22 to 27, 34 to 37, 39, 40 and 41 to 52.
This file maps questions; it does not answer them. No fleet measurement was
taken, so no command is recorded; every number is an external claim with its URL.
