Scope: what organisations thought worth enforcing, read as testable requirements. Every number below was read from a fetched page in this session (fetch cache: `~/.cache/research-lang/exemplars/product/ux-codified-systems/`). Search snippets were not used as sources.

## Method and gaps

Web search budget was exhausted, so no further source hunting was possible.
- Axis note: rows 4-7 and 33 rest on web.dev Core Web Vitals pages (named in the brief) and WCAG 2.2 (the standard the systems cite); rows 3, 8, 34 and the Contested entries use NN/g, and row 25 uses ADA and EU pages. These are adjacent sources, not platform guidelines or public design systems; read them as context, and the platform-guideline evidence in each row stands alone.

## Headline findings

1. The big systems mostly do not state numbers. Of Fluent 2, GOV.UK, USWDS, Carbon, Primer, Atlassian and GNOME, only Atlassian, Carbon, Primer and Fluent state any contrast or timing figures, and none of the web systems states a target size. The numbers live in four places: Apple HIG, Android/Material, Microsoft Windows docs, and WCAG 2.2. A rule that cites "the design systems" for numbers has nothing to cite.
2. Target size has three live numbers (24 CSS px WCAG AA, 44 pt Apple, 48 dp Material, plus Windows 40x40 px at 135 PPI and 1.0x scale, and Vercel's 44 px mobile) and a fourth axis, spacing between targets. An agent must be told which one applies to which surface.
3. Performance thresholds are stable and dated (LCP 2.5 s, INP 200 ms, CLS 0.1 at p75; poor at 4 s, 500 ms, 0.25), and they are field metrics, so a rule cannot verify them from a diff; it can verify the causes (image dimensions, font preload, long tasks).
4. Loading-state timing is contested between Primer (nothing under 1 s), Vercel (show-delay 150-300 ms, minimum visible 300-500 ms) and the Nielsen limits (0.1 s, 1 s, 10 s).
5. Legal and regulatory dates moved: US ADA Title II compliance date was pushed (April 2026 interim final rule), WCAG 3 is still a Working Draft (10 September 2026), the EU accessibility act has applied since 28 June 2025.
