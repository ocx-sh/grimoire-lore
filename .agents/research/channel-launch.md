# Launch venues and launch shape

## Verdict

1. **A launch is a gate check, not a campaign.** The measurable record does not show that any launch shape (single day, launch week, sequential venues) outperforms another for a small developer tool. No source measures it ([launch-venue-gates 2.8](channel-launch/launch-venue-gates.md), [launch-shape-evidence 6](channel-launch/launch-shape-evidence.md)). The wave-2 search for venue outcomes beyond stars did not change this (Verdict 13). What can be checked are each venue's stated entry gates, the link target, the staffing of the comment window, and the order in which URLs go live. The ruleset encodes those and nothing else. Where a rule rests only on practitioner or vendor practice, it is a SHOULD.
2. **Default shape for the fleet: rolling cadence plus single launch days.** The fleet already ships a release roughly every 3 days on its flagship surfaces (`product-audit/distribution-posture.md:66`). Supabase runs 8 to 13 blog posts per launch week against a median of 1 in other weeks ([launch-shape-evidence 3](channel-launch/launch-shape-evidence.md)). That figure shows what a week costs at Supabase's scale. It does not show that a week works: launch weeks are defined by their post clusters, so the cluster test cannot be evidence of effect. The threshold of five released items for a launch week (CH-LAUNCH-10) is an owner-adjustable heuristic with no source behind the number.
3. **Venue order (SHOULD, practitioner and vendor practice):** release in production, then the canonical record live, then one launch-day community venue, then social posts that link the canonical record, then free directories and awesome-list entries over the following weeks. Product Hunt is optional and comes last. The order rests on Supabase and Bucket posts, not on effect data (CH-LAUNCH-08). The one part a venue states, that the community venue links the runnable product and not the post, is a MUST (CH-LAUNCH-15).
4. **Venue fit for a free OSS CLI.** The default set is Show HN, DevHunt (free listing), Console.dev (by a human email) and live topic awesome lists entered at item level by a human (Verdict 14). Product Hunt is in the set only with two people on comment duty. Console.dev has two lanes on its [selection criteria](https://console.dev/selection-criteria) page (undated, "Copyright Console 2026", fetched 2026-11-27; [followup-q0](channel-launch/followup-q0.md)):
   - "Interesting tools": "Each week we feature and review 2-3 interesting developer tools". No version gate is stated.
   - "Betas": "the release must be pre 1.0 and/or have an appropriate label in the version number", or a "beta" or "preview" word on the site, release notes or docs. "Any GA or stable releases are not eligible."

   So the 1.0 tag closes only the Betas lane. Submission is "Email hello@console.dev"; no fee is stated. Documented gap: whether the Tools lane reviews a tool that is already at 1.0 on first submission. The page states no gate either way. The one data point is npkill, whose `latest` on npm was `1.0.0` (published 2026-11-12) when the Tools feed reviewed it on 2026-11-26. Closing the gap needs a human email to Console.dev.

   Default exclusions, each with its evidence status as fetched 2026-11-27:
   - BetaList: paid only ("All submissions are paid", [FAQ](https://betalist.com/faq)). Confirmed.
   - awesome-selfhosted: a standalone CLI is not a self-hosted service ([CONTRIBUTING](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/awesome-selfhosted/awesome-selfhosted-data/master/CONTRIBUTING.md)). Confirmed.
   - OpenSourceAlternative.to: needs a proprietary product to replace and a self-hosted tool ([submit](https://opensourcealternative.to/submit)). Confirmed.
   - Launch HN: YC startups only ([yli](https://news.ycombinator.com/yli.html)). Confirmed.
   - Uneed: the free launch is vote-gated. Uneed's own [submit page](https://uneed.best/submit-a-tool) names "the launch-day upvote score a free launch needs to stay published and keep its do-follow backlink", inside its prefilled "Ask AI" prompt links, and prints no number. The score of 10 is competitor-claimed ([DevHunt guide](https://devhunt.org/launch-platforms), vendor data about a competitor, checked 2026-11-24) and unconfirmed. This exclusion is a SHOULD.
   - Awesome lists that are archived or merged no PR in the 90 days before the plan date (CH-LAUNCH-17). Measured 2026-11-27 ([followup-q3](channel-launch/followup-q3.md)). This exclusion is a SHOULD.

   Not excluded by default, scope unverified, read on plan day:
   - selfh.st: the [submit page](https://selfh.st/submit/) states no criteria ("Self-hosted news, content, updates, launches, events, and more"). A human decides.
   - AlternativeTo: its [FAQ](https://alternativeto.net/faq/) (read through WebFetch; `curl` returns 403) says "We only list things someone would plausibly come to AlternativeTo looking for an alternative to". It does not require a proprietary product, so the gates dive's exclusion (`launch-venue-gates.md:84`) is not reproduced. Fit for a CLI is a plan-day judgement.
5. **Resolved: what the launch-day venue links to.** The shape dive says social posts link the canonical post. The gates dive and the HN contract say Show HN must link "something other people can play with", and that blog posts are off-topic ([showhn.html](https://news.ycombinator.com/showhn.html)). Product Hunt encourages linking straight to the product ([PH 17734646](https://help.producthunt.com/en/articles/17734646-8-launch-faq-and-myths)). Decision: community launch venues link the runnable target (landing page or repository), CH-LAUNCH-15. Social posts link the canonical record. Both rules hold because they govern different URL fields.
6. **Resolved: install command on the landing versus one click away.** The gates dive made "install command on the landing" a MUST for HN and PH, but states that this is its own decision and not a venue rule. Only 7 of 25 exemplar landings put the command in the hero (`product-audit/exemplar-landing-pages.md:211`), and site-shape refuses to call its absence a defect (`product-audit/site-shape.md:216`). Decision: a working install command for the launched product must be on the target or one click away (MUST). The command on the landing itself is not required. Under this decision grimoire.rs passes.
7. **Resolved: canonical record.** The shape dive wants an owned-domain post per headline plus a changelog entry that links back to it. The fleet has no blog or feed (`product-audit/distribution-posture.md:39,238`). Its changelogs are generated from conventional commits (`distribution-posture.md:41`), and one repository forbids hand edits to `CHANGELOG.md` (`grimoire-rs/vscode/CLAUDE.md:57-61`, via `product-audit/config-inventory.md:374`). Decision: the canonical record is one dated, stable URL the team controls. A GitHub Release page qualifies if its body's first line is a prose summary sentence. The record links the release; the changelog is never hand-edited to link back. The pairing rule is dropped: the benchmark company itself pairs 0 of 15 headlines ([launch-shape-evidence 4](channel-launch/launch-shape-evidence.md)). The rule is a SHOULD, because the evidence is one vendor's practice (CH-LAUNCH-09).
8. **Resolved: the launch goal counter.** The gates dive proposed GitHub stars at T+0, 24 h, 48 h and 7 d. The GitHub sibling dive found that stars include fake stars and cannot show removals ([channel-github/gh-signal-validity.md](channel-github/gh-signal-validity.md), GHSV-03). Decision: the goal uses the GHSV-16 field format, one counter from the GHSV-02 list and the GHSV-15 28-day baseline. Stars are reported, never targeted. Whether to add site analytics is a marketing-measurement decision, not a launch rule.
9. **Resolved: HN posting time.** The gates dive reports two timing results:
   - [danfking](https://danfking.github.io/blog/2026/04/23/show-hn-by-the-numbers/) (2026-04-23): Monday 00:00 UTC is the best single slot.
   - [arXiv 2511.04453](https://arxiv.org/html/2511.04453v1) (2025-11-06): 12-17 UTC posts gain more stars.

   The communities dive measured 75,929 Show HN posts. The only result that holds in both halves of its window is "avoid 06:00-10:59 UTC" (`channel-communities/com-hn-contract.md` summary). Decision: the plan avoids that window and names no "best slot". The best-slot claims are single-author picks that agree with neither the other source nor the larger set.
10. **Out of this topic:**
    - Show HN title rules, repost rules and the maker-comment contract belong to CH-COM ([com-hn-contract](channel-communities/com-hn-contract.md)).
    - Lobsters account rules belong to CH-COM ([com-venue-rules](channel-communities/com-venue-rules.md)).
    - Release-body shape belongs to CH-GH ([gh-release-announcement](channel-github/gh-release-announcement.md)).
    - The generic "agent never posts" boundary belongs to the agent-boundary topic ([core-platform-labels](channel-agent-boundary/core-platform-labels.md)).
    - The install path itself belongs to installer-script.
    - Site analytics belong to the marketing sibling's measurement topic.

    This file cites those topics and does not restate them.
11. **Documented gap in the grounding audits: a fleet launch plan exists.** The audits report no launch material. The grimoire repository holds a grim 1.0 promotion plan, committed 2026-11-17 (`git -C grimoire-rs/grimoire log -1 --format='%h %cs' -- .agents/plans/meta-plan_promotion_1_0.md` prints `af55322b 2026-11-17`). Its objective is "Ship 1.0 with a public surface that survives a Show HN" (`grimoire-rs/grimoire/.agents/plans/meta-plan_promotion_1_0.md:161`). It sequences Console.dev, then the 1.0 tag, then "Show HN + same-day" (same file, lines 225-240), and ranks ten venues (`grimoire-rs/grimoire/.agents/research/research_promotion_positioning.md:361-374`). The "Applied" rows below are measured against it.
12. **Resolved: what counts as a launch-day venue (CH-LAUNCH-06).** The rule never defined "community venue", so the grim 1.0 plan's day-0 items could not be classified ([followup-q1](channel-launch/followup-q1.md)). Decision: every channel gets one `venue_class`, set by one observable property, whether strangers reply in public to the team's post:
    - `community`: Show HN, PH and users.rust-lang.org announcements. The forum takes replies on every topic. Of the 20 newest announcement topics, 19 had 0 replies and 1 had 1 ([category 6](https://users.rust-lang.org/c/announcements/6), fetched 2026-11-27). No page states a duty to answer, so classing the forum as `community` is an inference. It is the safe direction: it costs one day of spacing.
    - `editorial`: Changelog News and Console.dev. An editor decides "if and when your submission gets published" ([changelog.com/news/submit](https://changelog.com/news/submit), undated, fetched 2026-11-27). No comment surface was found on two Changelog News pages (`grep -ioc comment` printed 0 on `/news` and on `/news/66`). An editorial item has no schedulable launch day.
    - `owned`: the canonical record and any mirror of it, such as a DEV.to copy. DEV's [terms](https://dev.to/terms) (undated, fetched 2026-11-27) bar a post that "cannot simply point to an external link". The mirror rule is CH-LAUNCH-16.

    A finding beyond classification: the grim plan's "Zero gatekeeping" line for the forum is stale. The moderators muted the announcements category for everyone on 2026-09-10 ([thread 142378](https://users.rust-lang.org/t/announcement-topic-category/142378), fetched 2026-11-27).
13. **Documented gap: no measured venue outcome beyond stars ([followup-q2](channel-launch/followup-q2.md), answered partly).** On 2026-11-27, no fetched source compared Show HN, PH and DevHunt on installs, retained users or contributors for a developer CLI. What exists:
    - [arXiv 2506.12643](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.12643) (v1 2025-06-14) is the one study with a non-star outcome. It covers Show HN only and AI repositories only: 163 projects, one month before versus one month after submission. Forks (p = 0.004) and new contributors (p = 0.021) rose significantly. The paper prints no effect sizes.
    - [arXiv 2603.07919](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.07919) (2026-03-09) is a field experiment on new Python packages. It found "no discernible impact on downloads" from bought stars. This is a reason to doubt that a star spike becomes installs.
    - DevHunt's only outcome claim ("2-5x") sits on a vendor page that returns HTTP 410 and has no archived snapshot. It is unread and unused.
    - npm download counts are counts of served tarballs and include build servers and mirrors ([npm blog](https://blog.npmjs.org/post/92574016600/numeric-precision-matters-how-npm-download-counts-work.html), 2014-07-22, vendor). They are a weak stand-in for installs.

    Closing the gap needs a dataset that joins launch events to registry download series and to contributor history. PH data needs an authenticated API token, which this program does not use. Consequences:
    - CH-LAUNCH-08 stays SHOULD.
    - CH-LAUNCH-04 admits venues by scope, not by outcome.
    - CH-LAUNCH-11 is unchanged.
    - The grim plan's "Highest leverage" ranking (`research_promotion_positioning.md:367`) and its "roughly 200–800 stars" (line 389) have no source.

    The follow-up proposed a rule against venue-effect claims. It is not added (Dropped table).
14. **Resolved: which awesome lists are worth an item ([followup-q3](channel-launch/followup-q3.md)).** The liveness was measured on 2026-11-27 through the public GitHub API, unauthenticated (vendor data):
    - Alive (merged PRs in 90 days): rust-unofficial/awesome-rust (295), hesreallyhim/awesome-claude-code (69, mostly bot merges of approved issues; 532 issues closed), punkpeye/awesome-mcp-servers (1912), VoltAgent/awesome-agent-skills (188).
    - Dead: agarrharr/awesome-cli-apps (archived), plus four lists with 0 merges in 90 days: alebcay/awesome-shell, e2b-dev/awesome-ai-agents, jamesmurdza/awesome-ai-devtools and bradAGI/awesome-cli-coding-agents. These four hold 154 to 1198 open PRs.
    - No maintained general package-manager list exists. The place for a package manager is the "Package Managers" section of awesome-rust.

    The fleet fails awesome-rust's gate today ("stars > 50 | downloads > 2000"). ocx has 20 stars and grimoire has 17, and neither has a crate of its own (CH-LAUNCH-18). awesome-claude-code takes recommendations by web form only, and they "must be created by human beings", which confirms CH-LAUNCH-12. awesome-mcp-servers invites agent PRs. The fleet boundary in CH-LAUNCH-12 still holds there unless a human overrides it for that list.

## The ruleset

Severity: MUST, SHOULD or CONSIDER. Two rules have a primary artifact:
- the **launch plan**: the file the `launch-plan` skill emits, holding one table row per venue and per step, with an `actor` column (`agent` or `human`) and a `## Venue facts` section;
- the **link target**: the URL a venue's URL field will hold.

Every venue page cited here prints no absolute date. "Fetched 2026-11-27" is the only anchor, so each rule that rests on such a page re-checks it at plan time (CH-LAUNCH-13).

## AI-agent failure modes

Ordered by expected cost to the team. Frequency is not measured, and no item below cites an observed agent output unless it says so.

**A. Template recall instead of fetched rules**
1. Writes "upvote us", "help us hit #1" or "support us on PH" into the launch tweet, the Discord announcement or the README. Hypothesised, not observed: no agent output in this research shows the line, and the fleet's existing launch plan contains none. Rule: CH-LAUNCH-05.
2. Proposes a launch week because launch-week posts dominate advice content, then fills the week with five thin items. Rule: CH-LAUNCH-10.
3. Quotes a character limit, a price or a "best hour" from memory: BetaList 75/700, "post Tuesday 9am ET", DevHunt prices. Observed once in the research record (the scout rows, `launch-venue-gates.md:130,132`). Rule: CH-LAUNCH-13.
4. Treats the sindresorhus/awesome list-submission gates (30 days, CC0, 4 reviews) as rules for adding an item to a topic list. Rule: CH-LAUNCH-12 check 4.

**B. Wrong URL in the wrong field**
5. Puts the announcement blog post in the Show HN or PH URL field, because the shape template says "link the post". Rule: CH-LAUNCH-15.
6. Links a docs page whose first install command installs a different product. Rule: CH-LAUNCH-03.

**C. Release automation leaking into launches**
7. Hooks a "draft Show HN" step onto every release, which for this fleet is every few days. Rule: CH-LAUNCH-07.
8. Takes a GitHub Release whose body is a commit list as the canonical record. Rule: CH-LAUNCH-09.

**D. Measurement errors**
9. Reports a link-target failure, or a pass, from a local resolver override. Rule: CH-LAUNCH-02 (`--resolve` against a public resolver).
10. Sets stars or PH rank as the goal. Rule: CH-LAUNCH-11.
11. Fails a page or a README on a bare keyword: "Sign up" in a footer, or a product feature called "upvote". Observed in this revision on the grimoire-vscode README. Rules: CH-LAUNCH-02 step 4, CH-LAUNCH-05.

**E. Crossing a human boundary**
12. Opens awesome-list PRs in a batch with `gh pr create`, or ticks awesome-selfhosted's "done by a human" box. Rule: CH-LAUNCH-12.
13. Picks the org account for PH, or schedules a 12:01 AM PT launch with nobody awake in the team's zone. Rules: CH-LAUNCH-14, CH-LAUNCH-06.
14. Admits or excludes a venue by its name alone (AlternativeTo, selfh.st) without reading its scope. Rule: CH-LAUNCH-04.

**F. Added in wave 2.** These are numbered on from 14 so that earlier references stay valid.

15. Reads a two-lane venue as one gate, and sets a false deadline or a false exclusion. Observed in the fleet's plan, which turned Console.dev's Betas sentence into "pre-1.0 tools only. 18. Picks an archived or dead awesome list because it ranks high by stars, such as awesome-cli-apps at 20661 stars. Hypothesised. Rule: CH-LAUNCH-17.

## What an agent must ask a human

- Whether to launch at all; which product; which venue and which day. Who staffs each block of the comment window, in which time zone, and for how many hours on Show HN (`hn_duty_hours`, CH-LAUNCH-06).
- Whether a release counts as "major" for Show HN or for a PH relaunch (`owner-major:<ref>`, CH-LAUNCH-07).
- Any spending: BetaList plans, DevHunt launch and boosts, Uneed fast-track, OpenSourceAlternative.to fast review, PH ads. Any override of a default venue exclusion, and the verdict on an `unverified` venue (CH-LAUNCH-04).
- Every submission, claim, PR to a third-party list and attestation (CH-LAUNCH-12). Which personal account launches on PH (CH-LAUNCH-14).
- Whether grimoire.rs's install page should lead with grim's own installer or keep ocx first by design (CH-LAUNCH-03).
- Whether the canonical record is a new owned-domain news page or the GitHub Release page (CH-LAUNCH-09). The first is new owned-publishing work; the second needs the release template's summary line (CH-GH).
- The item count N for a launch week, if not 5 (CH-LAUNCH-10).
- Which Console.dev lane to use, and who sends the email (CH-LAUNCH-04, CH-LAUNCH-12).
- Whether grim is "specific to Claude Code" for awesome-claude-code's required checkbox. Whether to argue "equivalent popularity" to awesome-rust before the 50-star gate is met (CH-LAUNCH-18). Any override of a dead-list exclusion, or of the agent boundary on a list that invites agents, such as awesome-mcp-servers (CH-LAUNCH-12, CH-LAUNCH-17).
- The day of the users.rust-lang.org post, which must be day +1 or later after Show HN (CH-LAUNCH-06), and who owns it.
- Weekday or weekend on PH. PH says only that weekends "can suit smaller teams" ([17734355](https://help.producthunt.com/en/articles/17734355-3-set-your-goals-and-pick-your-launch-date)), and no data measures it.
