## Summary

- **SH-CI is not clean.** No rule's normative content (MUST/SHOULD, the shell semantics) was overturned, but 7 checks are wrong on real files and 2 classes are missing. Amend SH-CI-05, 13, 17, 19, 24, 28 and 30 by ID; add SH-CI-31 and SH-CI-32. Details in [section 2](#2-verdict-not-clean-and-where).
- **The widened SH-CI-30 grep has 0% precision on held-out code.** 27 hits over 64 trees and 3,936 files, none a real `A || B && C` bug: 9 are GitHub `if:`/`runs-on:` expressions (where `&&` binds tighter than `||`), 4 are `||` before a `[[ a && b ]]`, 14 are gh-aw generated shell. A revised pattern drops all 27 and still hits the fleet's one real bug and all 5 planted variants ([section 5](#5-sh-ci-30-the-widened-grep-does-not-keep-its-precision)).
- **`gl-extract.py` crashes on 10 of 21 real GitLab pipeline files** (`!reference` tags in 9, a `spec:`/`---` two-document header in 1) and the documented pipeline `gl-extract.py ... | shellcheck -s sh -` then exits 0: a silent false green. It also aborts ShellCheck on a job named `shellcheck`, floods SC2317 after any `exit`, and feeds PowerShell jobs to `sh`. `gl-extract2.py` (fixture) fixes all four, each watched red on a plant and green on its twin.
- **GitLab Windows-runner jobs are PowerShell, not the image's shell.** inkscape's `.windows` jobs produced SC1066/SC2283/SC1036 parse errors under `-s sh`. SH-CI-28 needs a clause: tags or `extends` naming a Windows runner route the body to PS-* rules, not ShellCheck.
- **SH-CI-24's first stage keys on `fromjson(` anywhere in a file.** On held-out it listed 13 files of which 3 hold a dynamic matrix dimension, flagged 2 static-matrix lines in ruff, and missed tokio's `- ${{ needs.get-latest-kernel-version.outputs.kernel_version }}` list-item dimension. Anchoring stage 1 on the `matrix:` block lists 4 files, all 4 true.
- **SH-CI-23 on authored files: 45 of 49 hits are real run-body expansions (92%); 4 are not shell** (action inputs, a matrix list item). The generated-file exemption is recognisable: the header marker `^# .*(DO NOT EDIT|automatically generated|autogenerated|auto-generated)` found 4 of 4 generated held-out workflows, 0 false marks in 113 files, and the fleet's cargo-dist and mirror files too.
- **SH-CI-08 fires on 91 lines in 5 of 9 repos and 80% of the variables are literals** (94 of 117 uses). One chain is real: tokio's kernel version flows from a `curl` output through a matrix and `with:` into `uring-kernel-version-test.yml:102`. Keep the MUST, add a Limits note.
- **Every other SH-CI rule held**: 0 hits for SH-CI-09, 11, 14, 17b, 18, 26, 27, 29; 0 bash 4+ constructs in 137 macOS run steps; 0 composite-action violations; SH-CI-01 finds 26 genuine pipeline-without-pipefail steps in 6 repos.
- **SH-CI-17's verify grep has a false positive** (a task named `pipefail` after `shopt:`, go-task's own testdata) and the rule has no trigger: all 3 Taskfile repos have 0 `set:` keys and about 8 shell pipelines. Proposed SH-CI-32 supplies the trigger.
- **SH-CI-13's checker misses 8 bash 4+ constructs** that bash 3.2 rejects (measured on 3.2, 4.0, 4.4, 5.3): `${x^}`, `${x,}`, `${x^^pat}`, `[[ -v X ]]`, `|&`, `;;&`, `${x@Q}`, `shopt -s globstar` (and `declare -g`, `printf '%(..)T'`, `coproc`).
- **actionlint recurses into `.github/workflows/<subdir>/`**, GitHub does not read it as workflows. cli/cli keeps a 5,000-line eval log there: actionlint reports 6 syntax errors on it and three SH-CI greps hit it. Enumerate with `find .github/workflows -maxdepth 1` (SH-CI-31).
- **actionlint 1.7.12 default severity is not attainable on 2026 workflows**: 243 findings over 9 repos, 34 of them not ShellCheck (new `concurrency.queue`, `copilot-requests` permission, `field_added` event, custom runner labels), and 4 SC2193/SC2309 artefacts caused by `${{ }}` placeholders in comparisons; the `env:` route removes the artefact.
- **zizmor 1.30.1 at medium covers what the greps miss and vice versa**: it flagged `github.event.pull_request.head.repo.full_name` (arduino `check-certificates.yml:51`) which the SH-CI-07 field list does not name, and it shows `steps.*.outputs` only at informational (97 template-injection findings there, 5 at medium).
- **`shell: msys2 {0}` is a bash with `-leo pipefail`** (setup-msys2 `main.js:339`) and actionlint skips ShellCheck on it. SH-CI-05's reading aid lists it as a template to check; it is a known-good shim with an unlinted body.
- **Taskfile and workflow `run:` bodies hide in `with:` inputs** (tokio `vmactions/freebsd-vm` `prepare:` and `run:`); the SH-CI-07 greps catch the continuation lines but not a one-line `prepare: "... ${{ inputs.x }}"`.
- **PCRE backtracking limits bite multi-line `-Pz` checks on real files** (my first SH-CI-24 pattern failed silently on 9 real files with `exceeded PCRE's backtracking limit`; possessive quantifiers fixed it). The existing SH-CI greps produced 0 such errors on the 113 files.

### 4. SH-CI-23 and SH-CI-24: false-positive rate and the generated-file exemption

**SH-CI-23 on authored (non-generated) files.** 56 lines in 7 repos. 7 are in generated files (6 in the two gh-aw lock files, 1 in ruff's cargo-dist `release.yml:300`). Of the 49 authored, 45 sit inside a `run:`/`script:` scalar (`classify.py`, YAML-composed line ranges) and 4 do not:

| Hit | Where | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| `nightly-oss.yml:113` (goreleaser) | URL inside an action input | not shell, FP |
| `CICD.yml:449,450` (bat) | `with:` of a release action | not shell, FP |
| `ci.yml:1401` (tokio) | matrix list item | not shell, but the entry of a taint chain (F-05) |

False-positive rate (a hit that is not a shell-body expansion): 4/49 = 8.2%, 3/49 = 6.1% if the tokio item is counted as a true signal. The semantic rate is higher: most of the 45 carry repo-derived values (bat's `needs.crate_metadata.outputs.name` is read from `Cargo.toml`), a minority are tag-derived (arduino `release-go-task.yml:125,269,337` `TAG=${{ needs.create-release-artifacts.outputs.version }}`, produced by `task general:get-version` at `:90`). The rule is stated on syntax by design ([shell-ci.md SH-CI-23](shell-ci.md)); the fix is one `env:` line, so the rate is acceptable.

**SH-CI-24 as written.** Stage 1 (`grep -rliP -e 'fromjson\(...'`) listed 13 files (arduino 2, ruff 7, cli 3, goreleaser 1); three hold an expression-built dimension (arduino `test-go-task.yml:90`, ruff `sign-release-binaries.yml:303,369`, cli `acceptance.yml:31`), and one more exists that the grep cannot see (tokio `ci.yml:1401`). Stage 2 printed 4 lines: arduino `test-go-task.yml:110,113` (`${{ matrix.tests }}`, `tests: ${{ fromJSON(needs.tests-collector.outputs.tests-data) }}`) are true; ruff `build-docker.yml:85,221` are false (a static `platform:`/`image-mapping:` matrix in a file whose `if:` and `with:` call `fromJson(inputs.plan)`). Line-level FP rate 2/4 = 50%; file-level precision of stage 1 = 3/13 = 23%, recall 3/4.

**Revision** (run, H1): anchor stage 1 on the `matrix:` block with possessive quantifiers and `\1` indentation.

```
grep -rlPzZ -e '(?im)^([ ]*)matrix:[ ]*+(?:\$\{\{[^\n]*?\b(?:needs|steps|github\.event|inputs)\.|\n(?:\1[ ]++[^\n]*+\n)*?\1[ ]++(?:[A-Za-z0-9_-]++:[ ]*+|-[ ]++)\$\{\{[ ]*+(?:fromjson\([ (]*+)?(?:needs|steps|github\.event|inputs)\.)' --include='*.yml' --include='*.yaml' .github/workflows | xargs -r -0 grep -Pn -e '^(?!\s*(?:-\s+)?(?!run:)[A-Za-z_][A-Za-z0-9_.-]*:\s).*\$\{\{[^}]*\bmatrix\.'
```

Held-out result: stage 1 lists 4 files (ruff `sign-release-binaries.yml`, cli `acceptance.yml`, tokio `ci.yml`, arduino `test-go-task.yml`), 4 of 4 true, recall 4/4; stage 2 prints arduino 2 (true) and tokio 17 (static `matrix.os`-style dimensions in the same file; the dynamic `kernel_version` is only used in `name:` and `with:`), ruff 0 and cli 0 (both use the compliant `TARGET: ${{ matrix.platform.target }}` env route). Honest cost: stage 2 stays file-level, so a file that mixes a dynamic and many static dimensions lists all of them and the reader keeps the dynamic one. A dimension-aware variant (extract the dimension name with `\K`, then `xargs -I{} grep 'matrix\.{}'`) was built and dropped: it collides across files (ruff `platform` is dynamic in one file and static in another, 8 false lines).

**Generated-file exemption is recognisable.** The marker `-i -e '^# .*DO NOT EDIT' -e '^# .*automatically generated' -e '^# .*autogenerated' -e '^# .*auto-generated'` over `.github/workflows` lists exactly: ruff `release.yml` ("autogenerated by dist", [ruff@a5aee680b11d:.github/workflows/release.yml:1](https://github.com/astral-sh/ruff/blob/a5aee680b11d2190fbb6a72008047ae281dd2d18/.github/workflows/release.yml)), cli `dependabot-triage.lock.yml`, `issue-triage.lock.yml` and `agentics-maintenance.yml` ("automatically generated by gh-aw ... DO NOT EDIT", line 3); 4 of 4 generated, 0 false marks in 113 files (cli `check-easyjson`-style comments saying "generated" in arduino `check-easyjson.yml` do not match the `^# .*` marker forms). Fleet cross-check (read only): ocx and grimoire `release.yml` carry "autogenerated by dist"; e2e-mirror 5 of 5 files carry "DO NOT EDIT". The exempting pipeline `grep -rLZ -i -e ... .github/workflows | xargs -r -0 grep -HPn ...` left authored hits ripgrep 4, cli 4, bat 23, tokio 1, goreleaser 2, arduino 15, ruff 0 (49, equal to the authored count). Limit: gh-aw generated YAML is large (cli's two lock files are 120 KB each) and carries embedded shell that fails SH-CI-30 and SH-CI-23 by design; the marker is the only handle, so do not edit those files.

### 5. SH-CI-30: the widened grep does not keep its precision

The 27 held-out hits: **9 are GitHub expressions** (coder `nightly-gauntlet.yml:17` and `ci.yaml:548` `runs-on:`, pixi `docs.yml:64,112` `if:`, and 5 continuation lines in gh-aw files such as cli `dependabot-triage.lock.yml:485`). In GitHub's expression grammar `&&` binds tighter than `||`: [the operators table lists `&&` above `||`](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/github/docs/main/content/actions/reference/workflows-and-actions/expressions.md), and act (an emulation of the expression engine, not a hosted runner) ran `${{ true || false && false }}` as true and skipped `${{ (true || false) && false }}` (`act-prec`). In a shell the same text is left-associative ([POSIX XCU 2.9.3: "&& and || shall have equal precedence and shall be evaluated with left associativity"; the standard's own example `true || echo foo && echo bar` writes only `bar`](https://pubs.opengroup.org/onlinepubs/9799919799/utilities/V3_chap02.html)). **4 are `||` before a `[[ a && b ]]`** or inside one (bash-it `uninstall.sh:14,16`, `completion/available/projects.completion.bash:30`, sdkman `sdkman-env-helpers.sh:72`): the middle class excludes `]` but not `[[`, and `&&` inside `[[ ]]` is test syntax. **14 are gh-aw generated `bash -c '... && ... || true && ...'`** lines (cli ×4, winget-cli ×4, vscode-powershell ×6).

Revised pattern (run, H3 and `s30run.sh`):

On the plants (`plants/s30`): `bad/` 5 of 5 lines red (apk/apt on alpine, `test -d || mkdir && cd`, `rm || echo >&2 && exit 1`, a GitLab list item, a workflow `run:` block); `twin/` (grouped form, `if/elif`, four `[[ ]]` forms, GitHub `if:`/`runs-on:`/continuation expressions) 0 lines, where the old pattern gave 5; generated twin 0. ## Contested / evolving

- **SH-CI-08 as a MUST on syntax versus provenance** (as of 2026-10-11). GitHub's guidance prefers an intermediate environment variable and zizmor reports the env context only at low and informational, while 80% of held-out uses are literals. The program's stance (syntax rule, one-line fix, agents cannot decide provenance) holds for agent-written code; an adopter gate that fails on 91 lines will be argued down to "non-literal only". Trend: zizmor is moving toward provenance (confidence levels, `--persona`); keep the MUST and the Limits note.
- **SH-CI-23's cross-job taint.** 45 authored run-body hits, most repo-derived. The act result in N-act (taint survives a first safe hop) argues for syntax; the held-out population shows how noisy that is on tag- and Cargo-derived values. Trend: no consensus; both greps stay.
- **actionlint's schema lags GitHub.** `concurrency.queue`, `copilot-requests`, `field_added` and runner labels fail a default-severity actionlint 1.7.12 gate on gh-aw lock files. gh-aw lock files (generated, 120 KB each, DO NOT EDIT) are spreading (cli, winget-cli, vscode-powershell); they need an exclusion for actionlint, SH-CI-23/24/30 and SH-CI-07. Trend: more generated workflows, so the marker lister becomes a standing part of the gate.
- **GitLab dialect is not statically decidable** when `image:` is a variable (2 of 4 repos). Practice splits between "write POSIX sh" (the rule) and "pin a bash image and say so". No tool decides it; the cheap answer is `--sh` and a one-line `# bash image` note.
- **Taskfile pipefail.** Task's docs describe `set:` and `shopt:`; nobody in the three held-out repos uses either. Whether Task will default pipefail on is not announced; revisit when mvdan/sh or Task changes the default.
- **Windows Taskfile tools** (SH-CI-20/25) stay as shell-ci.md left them (SHOULD): the hosted-image PATH claim is source-read, still unmeasured on a runner.
