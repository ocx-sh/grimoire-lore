## What the audience penalises and rewards (evidence summary)

Penalised: anything that reads as marketing, unsupported uniqueness or speed claims, perceived insincerity ([Nordic APIs review of DuVander's book, 2021-07-01](https://nordicapis.com/book-review-developer-marketing-does-not-exist/)); dark patterns, deal pop-ups, unverifiable product claims, unfair competitor criticism ([PostHog handbook](https://posthog.com/handbook/marketing)); gamed popularity signals ([CMU, 2025-09-03](https://www.cs.cmu.edu/news/2025/fake-github-stars)).

Rewarded: practical, technically deep content that helps developers solve problems themselves; accurate interactive docs, clear pricing, status information; guides that say how not to use the product ([Nordic APIs review](https://nordicapis.com/book-review-developer-marketing-does-not-exist/)). Word of mouth by default and low-volume relevant outreach ([PostHog](https://posthog.com/handbook/marketing)). Long-form articles are the second most chosen way to find content (40.8%), behind recommendation lists (47.6%) ([Stack Overflow Developer Survey 2025](https://survey.stackoverflow.co/2025/)).
