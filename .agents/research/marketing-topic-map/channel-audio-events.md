## Boundary with existing catalog

docs-quality covers documentation page types and plain English; css-theming covers tokens and dark mode. Nothing in talks, streams, podcasts or webinars is covered. Slide and recording accessibility overlaps with WCAG items elsewhere but no rule applies them to decks or recordings, so rows marked "no" or "partial" are open.

## Candidate topics
