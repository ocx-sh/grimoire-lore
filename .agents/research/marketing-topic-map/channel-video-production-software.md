# Producing video about software: landscape scout

Scope: which questions an agent must get right when it plans, scripts, produces or verifies video about software. Method: pages were opened and read through WebFetch on 2026-11-27. Search was unavailable (the session search budget was exhausted), so discovery relied on known canonical URLs. Several targets could not be read and are listed under "Not verified". No fact below comes from memory.

## Headline findings

1. The agent-checkable surface is mostly file-level: caption file present and in a format the platform accepts, WCAG 1.2.x coverage, loudness, text size, chapter timestamps, format and size limits, alt/poster/autoplay attributes, disclosure text. The creative surface (hook, script) has weaker evidence, mostly vendor data.
2. The fleet already runs a terminal-footage pipeline (asciicast files played by an asciinema player). It has no video files, no captions, no YouTube embeds. So the first adopter need is the terminal-recording and web-embed path, not studio video.
3. Three legal and accessibility dates moved recently and are easy to get wrong: EU AI Act Article 50 applies from 2 August 2026; the ADA Title II dates were extended by an April 2026 interim final rule; WCAG 2.2 Understanding pages carry different update dates (1.2.2 captions: 10 August 2026; 1.2.3/1.2.5 audio description: 9 March 2026; 1.4.3 contrast: 4 October 2026; WAI media guide: 17 September 2024), so any rule citing WCAG text needs a per-page date, not one shared date.
4. Vendor benchmark data on length conflicts by vendor and category (see Contested). A rule should state the category and the vendor, never a single "ideal length".
