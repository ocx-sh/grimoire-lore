# Landscape: search and answer-engine discoverability

## Scope and method

The corpus is Google Search Central, Bing Webmaster, the crawler documentation of OpenAI, Anthropic,
Perplexity and Google, the Princeton GEO paper, and vendor or practitioner studies (labelled as such).
Every platform fact below was fetched on 2026-11-27 with WebFetch or curl. Limits of this run:

- WebSearch was out of budget. Discovery went through known primary URLs and one changelog, so the
  landscape is wide on primary sources and thin on practitioner commentary.
- Aleyda Solis, Search Engine Land and Kevin Indig/Growth Memo returned 403 or a rate-limited safety
  check. Their positions are NOT represented except through Ahrefs, Lily Ray and Bing material. A
  follow-up pass should fetch them.
- Most Google Search Central pages carry no visible date in the fetched text. Where a date is known it
  comes from the Search Central changelog (https://developers.google.com/search/updates), which lists
  entries by month and day; the changelog year is taken from the page the tool returned (2026 entries).
- The fetch tool summarises pages with a small model. Quotes are as returned; a rule author must
  re-open the page before encoding a number.
