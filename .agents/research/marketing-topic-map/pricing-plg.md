## What the corpus says (short)

- Pricing model drift: hybrid (subscription plus usage) is now the most common primary model, 37% in the 2026 survey, up from 25% a year earlier; 29% use AI credits and 33% more plan to within 6-12 months; median target gross margin on AI products is about 50% against 70-80%+ for SaaS; three in four companies changed pricing or packaging in the past year. Survey of 230 B2B software and AI companies, April-May 2026, published 2026-05-13: https://growthunhinged.com/p/the-state-of-b2b-monetization-in-2026
- 2025 edition (240+ companies, typical respondent $1-20M ARR, published 2025-06-04): flat-fee 29% to 22%, seat-based 21% to 15%, hybrid 27% to 41%; outcome-based pricing out of reach for about 95%: https://kylepoyar.substack.com/p/2025-state-of-b2b-monetization
- Freemium and trial benchmarks (1,000+ products, Lenny Rachitsky with Kyle Poyar and Pendo, 2023-08-01): freemium self-serve good 3-5%, great 6-8%; freemium sales-assist good 5-7%, great 10-15%; free trial good 8-12%, great 15-25%; developer-focused companies median 5%, about half of non-developer; measured per account over six months, "averages, not targets": https://www.lennysnewsletter.com/p/what-is-a-good-free-to-paid-conversion
- Activation (500+ responses, 2022-10-25): SaaS average 36%, median 30%; formula is users reaching the milestone divided by users completing signup; milestone should predict at least 2x retention; only about 6% of respondents had a time-bound milestone: https://www.lennysnewsletter.com/p/what-is-a-good-activation-rate
- Activation metric method (2022-11-08): brainstorm, regress against retention, then run experiments; "causal, not just correlative": https://www.lennysnewsletter.com/p/how-to-determine-your-activation
- Freemium as budget line: Lovable's head of growth calls the free tier "our marketing budget", reports double-digit free-to-paid, and says top-up credit repurchase is as high as subscription renewal (Stripe Sessions 2026 write-up, vendor-hosted, no stated page date): https://stripe.com/customers/lovable-elena-verna
