# YouTube: search, Shorts and policy - landscape scout

Method: every page below was opened with WebFetch on 2026-11-27 and read through
the tool's extraction prompt; no fact comes from a search snippet or memory. The
web-search budget was exhausted mid-run, so URLs were reached by direct fetch.
Many YouTube Help pages state no publication date (only "(c) 2026 Google"); the
Dated facts table says so rather than invent one. Extraction was done by a small
model, so two statements are flagged in "Contested" for a re-read by a deep-dive

shell command applies; the "how to check" column names the field or screen.
