# Software marketing: what changed 2024-11 to 2026-11

## Method and gaps

Method: pages opened with WebFetch. No local command produced a measurement, so
every number is quoted from the cited page, with the page's own sample and
method where it states them. `sources_fetched: 60` is the number of distinct
URLs opened in this repair pass that returned usable content; each is listed in
"Sources opened in this pass" at the end, and the count is re-runnable with the
command given there. A further 20 URLs in the tables were opened by the earlier
pass and were not re-opened now; they are marked "carried" in the same list and
are not in the 60. WebSearch was unavailable (session budget used up), so
discovery went through site indexes and links inside primary pages. That
limits recall. Treat every gap below as "not verified", never as "nothing
changed".

Open gaps after this pass:

- No primary LinkedIn page states post, document, video or newsletter limits. Five LinkedIn help pages were opened (a519839, a564143, a549044, a519848, a523414); none states a limit. Do not hard-code LinkedIn limits.
- No measured click-through rate for Google AI Mode exists in any source opened. Only a referral-rate figure (Similarweb, sample unstated) and citation-overlap studies were found.
- No primary evidence for external-link penalties on LinkedIn, X, Facebook or YouTube. Only format-level engagement data (Buffer, Socialinsider) was found, and both sources say or imply it is not proof of suppression.
- Safari 26: WebKit's feature post names the protections but does not state default scope (all browsing or Private Browsing). Still unresolved.
- TikTok AI-label rules: the support page returned only its title. Not read.
- Threads and Mastodon user data, and the Bluesky date of the counter: not obtained (counter page is undated).
- Early window (2024-11 to 2025-11) of the Google status dashboard: the dashboard lists only incidents from December 2025.

## What changed, by corpus item (one-paragraph readout)

AI search. Three independent families of evidence agree on direction and
disagree on size. Click-through drops where an AI Overview appears (Pew 2025;
Ahrefs 2026 English; Ahrefs France 2026). AI referrals to ordinary sites are
tiny (0.12% of visits in early 2025; under 1% of conversions in 2026
benchmarks). Google says nothing special is required. No source opened shows a
GEO tactic with a causal effect on current engines.

Social. Format data exists (documents and carousels beat link posts on
LinkedIn). Penalty-for-links evidence does not. Developer audiences are not
concentrated on any one microblog; YouTube, GitHub, Reddit and Stack Overflow
lead for community (Stack Overflow survey 2025).

Platform rules. YouTube, LinkedIn and Meta label AI media; the EU AI Act
transparency rules apply from 2026-08-02; the Product Hunt and Hacker News
rules forbid solicited votes and, for HN, generated text.
