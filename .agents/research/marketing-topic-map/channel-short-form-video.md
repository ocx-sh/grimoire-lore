# Short-form video for software and B2B — landscape scout

## Method and gaps (read first)

Repair pass over the first artifact. Every URL cited below was opened with WebFetch on 2026-11-27 and returned content that contains the stated fact, unless a row says the page does not carry it. A finding that "no platform publishes X" is recorded as a finding, with the page that was opened. Where a fetch returned only a title, a 404 or a client-rendered shell, the fact is NOT stated; it appears under "Gaps still open".

Fetch routes that worked: platform developer docs (TikTok, Meta, Google, LinkedIn on Microsoft Learn), TikTok ads help, Meta and YouTube help pages that render server-side. Routes that failed: Meta Business Help, Instagram Help, TikTok Support and LinkedIn Help (client-rendered, title only). Web search was available for part of the run; search snippets were used only to find pages, never as sources.

Measurements in this file (two, both arithmetic or extraction, nothing measured on a repository):

- The Google "Universal Safe Zones" PDF was downloaded by WebFetch and its 9 embedded JPEGs were extracted with `python3 -I` and a `re.finditer(rb'\xff\xd8\xff.*?\xff\xd9', data, re.S)` loop; the two largest were viewed. They show horizontal-ad overlays only, with no numbers.
- The combined safe rectangle in candidate 9 was computed with `python3 -I -c "t=max(240,288,round(.14*1920));b=max(660,672,round(.35*1920));l=max(120,48,round(.06*1080));r=max(180,192,round(.06*1080));print(t,b,l,r)"`, giving 288, 672, 120, 192.

Priority is for this project shape: open-source developer tools sold to engineers, a small team, no video staff, AI agents writing the config. "Check" means what a rule or skill could verify mechanically.
