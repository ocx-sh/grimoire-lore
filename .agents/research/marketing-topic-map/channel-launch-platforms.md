## Measurements

- Linear changelog gaps between the ten listed dates (30 Jul, 13 Aug, 20 Aug, 3 Sep, 14 Sep, 24 Sep, 8 Oct, 22 Oct, 5 Nov, 12 Nov 2026): `python3 -I` date subtraction over dates extracted by regex from `curl -sL https://linear.app/changelog`; result 14, 7, 14, 11, 10, 14, 14, 14, 7 days, mean 11.67.
- YC Launches feed: `curl -sL https://www.ycombinator.com/launches` returns JSON; `python3 -I` over `hits`: 20 hits, nbHits 3545, nbPages 50, vote counts min 3, median 8.5, max 32, 13 of 20 from Fall 2026 batch, created 2026-11-18 to 2026-11-25. Median 8.5 votes per YC launch post shows vote counts there are small; do not set vote targets from other venues.
