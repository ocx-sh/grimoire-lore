# LinkedIn paid distribution for B2B software: landscape map

## Method and gaps

LinkedIn's own help center (`linkedin.com/help/lms/answer/...`) fetched cleanly and is the primary source. Its pages state only a relative age ("Last updated: 4 months ago"), never a date, so every help-center fact below is dated "as fetched 2026-11-27, page age as shown". The Microsoft Learn LinkedIn Marketing API pages carry real dates and version monikers and are the best dated source.

Gaps, stated plainly:

- The web search budget was exhausted before any search ran. Every source was fetched by URL, so discovery was limited to links found on LinkedIn's own pages.
- `business.linkedin.com` and `linkedin.com/business/marketing/blog` returned HTTP 999 or 400 and could not be read. `b2binstitute.com` and `ehrenberg-bass.com` did not resolve.
- Consequently the B2B Institute 95-5 and creative-effectiveness primary research, and independent Thought Leader Ads performance evidence, were NOT read. Those candidates are marked as unverified leads. One vendor benchmark (Dreamdata) was read. Per-format CTR and CPC benchmarks were not found.
