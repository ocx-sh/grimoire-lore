# Community venue facts

Every venue clause, account gate and chat threshold the `community-post` skill uses sits in this file and nowhere else. Cite a row by its id in square brackets, such as `[CPHN-F04]`. A venue rule that is not in a row is not known: read the venue's own page in the same session (CH-COM-02) and never fill it in from memory.

Contents: [Dates and versions](#dates-and-versions) · [Rows](#rows)

## Dates and versions

- Fetched and verified: 2026-11-27. Every `verified` date is the agent's own fetch date. Rows on `support.reddithelp.com` and `www.reddit.com` were read from an archive copy dated 2026-08-28, and rows on `support-dev.discord.com` from one dated 2026-11-18, because the live pages answer scripts with 403. A human re-reads those hosts in a browser.
- Refresh cadence: every row carries `refresh_days` of 90, which is the cadence floor for this skill, or an `expires:` date for an event. `facts_check.py` in `channel-publishing` fails on the first row past `verified + refresh_days`. Re-fetch the page and compare it with `expect`. Never extend the date.
- Venue rules are also re-read on the day of a plan (CH-COM-02). The registry catches drift between plans, and the live page is the contract for this plan.
- Hacker News pages print no date. The `source_date` of a Hacker News row is the page's `Last-Modified` response header (a GET returns it, a HEAD returns 405), or the date of the moderator comment, or `none` when neither is known. Before each launch, diff the header of `newsguidelines.html`, `showhn.html`, `newsfaq.html` and `yli.html`, and re-read item 22336638 through `https://hacker-news.firebaseio.com/v0/item/22336638.json` for a new `Edit (` line.
- Re-check events: a new edit of the Hacker News tips, a new revision of the dev.to AI-assisted guidelines (a tiered revision was reported only by a mirror and was not read), the Hashnode terms and code of conduct (each changed twice in 2026), and the Discord message-indexing start. The Discord indexing row expires on 2027-01-15, the date after which the announced start has passed.
- Provenance: every row is `platform`, the venue's own page. No row is a vendor figure.
- Not in this file: base rates for Hacker News posts. They are a count made on 2026-11-27 over the Algolia API, not a venue clause, and they sit in the `CH-COM-10` row of `rules.md` with their window and sample size.
